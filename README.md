# Afcsc Heritage Journey

Please pay very close attention to this project brief before you start building.

I want you to understand that this is a completely new project, and I am expecting something significantly more impressive than an ordinary website or gallery.

I have attached the actual AFCSC logo, historical photographs and videos. Please inspect and use these assets in their correct sections. The official AFCSC logo must replace any generic shield or placeholder branding. Do not ignore the attached materials or substitute unrelated images.

My biggest priority is the animation and overall experience. I want beautiful, noticeable, smooth and professionally coordinated animations throughout the entire project, not just the welcome screen. Pay particular attention to the aircraft-inspired welcome animation, screen transitions, interactive timeline, photographic archive, video archive and touch interactions.

Please study the reference websites included in my brief and aim for that level of visual quality and storytelling, while creating an original design suitable for AFCSC.

Also, please do not make the mistake of filling the screens with ordinary cards and long scrolling pages. Visitors should be able to explore the exhibition easily through interactive navigation, animated storytelling, horizontal timelines and immersive viewing experiences.

Use a brighter, sophisticated military-inspired colour palette rather than making everything dark. Maintain the official institutional identity and make the design look premium.

Most importantly, build the actual working experience, not just attractive screenshots. Every button, navigation item, image viewer and interaction must function.

Please inspect the supplied assets, follow the complete brief carefully, and prioritise quality over rushing the design. I want a result that will genuinely impress visitors and judges.
